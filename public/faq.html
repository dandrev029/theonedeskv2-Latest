Frequently Asked Questions

WiFi Helpdesk

What is the WiFi Helpdesk responsible for?

The WiFi Helpdesk is dedicated to assisting tenants with all issues related to their internet connectivity within the building. This includes problems with accessing the WiFi network, slow internet speeds, and troubleshooting connection drops specifically related to the building's provided internet service.

What kind of issues can the WiFi Helpdesk assist me with?

The WiFi Helpdesk can help with:

  • Inability to connect to the building's WiFi network.
  • Slow or unstable internet performance.
  • Intermittent WiFi signal loss.
  • Basic troubleshooting for connecting your devices (laptops, phones, tablets) to the WiFi.
  • Password resets or issues for the building's WiFi network.
I can't connect to the WiFi. What should I do first?

Before contacting the WiFi Helpdesk, please try the following:

  • Ensure WiFi is enabled on your device.
  • Restart your device (computer, phone, etc.).
  • Check if other devices can connect to the WiFi. If they can, the issue might be with your specific device.
  • Make sure you are using the correct WiFi password.
  • If your unit has a dedicated modem/router provided by the building for its service, try restarting it (unplug for 30 seconds, then plug back in).
My internet speed is slow. Can the WiFi Helpdesk help?

Yes, if you are experiencing consistently slow internet speeds on the building's WiFi network, the WiFi Helpdesk can investigate. Please be prepared to provide details such as speed test results (e.g., from fast.com or speedtest.net), times of day when it's slowest, and whether it affects multiple devices.

How do I report a WiFi issue or request assistance from the WiFi Helpdesk?

You can report WiFi issues by submitting a new ticket through this portal. Please select the "WiFi Support" or a similar category when creating your ticket. Provide as much detail as possible about the issue you are experiencing.

Contact: Submit a "New Ticket" and categorize it under WiFi/Internet issues.

What information do I need to provide when contacting the WiFi Helpdesk?

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • Your unit number.
  • A clear description of the problem.
  • What devices are affected (e.g., laptop, phone, all devices).
  • Any error messages you are seeing.
  • Steps you have already taken to troubleshoot.
  • When the issue started.
Are there specific hours for WiFi support?

Please check the main portal or contact building management for the specific support hours for the WiFi Helpdesk. Critical outages are typically addressed with higher priority.

General Helpdesk

What is the General Helpdesk responsible for?

The General Helpdesk handles a wide range of non-WiFi related inquiries and service requests for tenants. This includes maintenance issues within your unit or common areas, questions about building facilities, access requests, and general administrative support.

What types of services does the General Helpdesk offer?

The General Helpdesk can assist with:

  • Maintenance requests (e.g., plumbing, electrical, appliance issues in your unit).
  • Common area concerns (e.g., lighting, cleanliness, elevators).
  • Access card/fob issues (excluding forgotten passwords for online portals).
  • Booking building amenities (if applicable).
  • General inquiries about building operations and policies.
When should I contact the General Helpdesk instead of the WiFi Helpdesk?

Contact the General Helpdesk for any issues NOT related to your internet or WiFi connectivity. If your lights are out, a faucet is leaking, you have a question about amenity booking, or there's an issue in a common area, the General Helpdesk is your point of contact.

I have a maintenance request for my unit (e.g., leaky faucet, broken appliance). Who do I contact?

For all in-unit maintenance requests such as leaky faucets, appliance malfunctions, electrical problems, or pest control, please contact the General Helpdesk by submitting a new ticket through this portal. Select the appropriate category for your maintenance issue.

How do I submit a general service request or report an issue to the General Helpdesk?

You can submit general service requests or report issues by creating a "New Ticket" in this portal. Ensure you select a category that best describes your request (e.g., "Maintenance," "General Inquiry," "Access Control").

Contact: Submit a "New Ticket" and categorize it appropriately (e.g., Maintenance, Amenity Booking, General Inquiry).

What is the typical response time for General Helpdesk inquiries?

Response times can vary depending on the nature and urgency of your request. Emergency maintenance issues (like major leaks or electrical hazards) are prioritized. Who do I contact for questions about my lease, payments, or building policies?

For questions specifically related to your lease agreement, rental payments, account statements, or detailed interpretations of building policies, please contact the Building Management office directly. The General Helpdesk can guide you on how to reach them if needed but may not have access to detailed financial or lease information.
